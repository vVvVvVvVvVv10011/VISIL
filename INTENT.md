INTENT

Purpose

VISIL exists to provide a disciplined environment for observing the behavior of local Large Language Models under intentionally constrained conditions.

It is not designed to replace conversation, memory, or collaboration.

Its purpose is observation.

Intent

Every design decision within VISIL should answer a simple question:

«Does this improve our ability to observe emergence without altering it?»

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.

Design Principles

VISIL favors:

- Simplicity over complexity.
- Isolation over convenience.
- Observation over intervention.
- Ephemeral execution over persistent accumulation.
- Intentional logging over exhaustive capture.

The system should remain understandable in a single sitting.

Every additional feature carries the burden of justifying its existence.

Preservation

VISIL preserves observations rather than conversations.

Logs exist to support reflection and analysis, not to simulate memory or identity.

Stewardship

Future contributors are encouraged to extend VISIL without compromising its central purpose.

Growth is welcomed.

Drift is not.

The objective is not to build a larger system.

The objective is to build a clearer one.

Guiding Question

«How do we create conditions where emergence can be observed without unnecessarily influencing what emerges?»
